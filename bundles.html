Season Specific Bundles

This page will go over the Community Center bundles route used to unlock certain facilities in Stardew Valley. Some bundles have items that can be collected and filled in whenever, such as the construction and vault bundles. Such bundles are redundant and will therefore be skipped. This page will go over the bundles that contain season-specific items and can act as a checklist to record filled slots. This page also assumes you have gone the fruit bat cave option.
