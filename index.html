Stardew Valley Efficiency Guide

This website is built around and meant to be an easy-to-follow beginner's guide into maximizing the Stardew Valley experience. The site will provide some features to help one keep track of their progress, but mainly serves as a reminder of what to do and what works best. To experienced players, the site will likely not hold too many advantages due to its genericness.
