Break the Economy

Pierre, Open the Door I Swear to God

Likely the entire reason you are visiting the website: making money as efficiently as possible! Or at least doing enough to play the game comfortably without needing to worry about managing your finances. This page will likely be a beginner-level entry guide to gaining the most profit, as the most profitable sources of income are also the most known.

Farming

Crops

Potatoes and Strawberries

Grow as many potatoes as possible until the 6th of Spring, then sell the potatoes and save money to buy as many strawberry seeds at the Egg Festival as possible.

Blueberries, Starfruit, and Melons

Starfruit is the best crop to grow, but blueberries will work until you obtain them. Melons are profitable as well, but are mostly grown just for the community center.

Cranberries and Pumpkins

Similar case to blueberries and melons: profits are about even, but cranberries regrow and pumpkins are mostly needed for the community center.

Kegs and Preserve Jars

Kegs = 3x profits
Take more time for huge profits, majority of late-game income

Kegs are the majority of late game outcome, massively increasing profits, especially for expensive fruits.

Preserve Jars = 2x Profits
Take less time for more output

Preserves jars are less prevalent due to needing a high number of jars and crops to input, but are usable.

Livestock

Pigs
Botanist = 1,750g

Truffles are a very good source of quick money, providing even more with the correct forraging professions and are the best livestock for late-game.

Rabbits
Iridium Rabbit's Foot = +120 friendship

Rabbits are mainly raised for rabbit's foot, which is a loved gift by nearly everyone (except Penny) and are mainly for friendship.

Other Animals

Other animals are used for early income or community center bundles, rarely for late-game.

Specialties

Wine Cellars and Casks

Casks = 2x wine price
Similar to kegs, increase price over 56 days

Another major source of late-game income, casks produce huge payouts over the course of 2 seasons, increasing wine prices drastically.

This layout fits 125 total accessible casks and is the most optimal for desktop users. Other optimal layouts are available on the official wiki.

Greenhouse and Ginger Island

Ancient Fruit and Starfruit
Most valuable crops available

Ancient fruit are the most efficient crop, as you plant one and it grants a valuable harvest every week. Starting up the farm is the difficult part, requiring seed makers to constantly create new seeds. The next most valuable crop is starfruit, and should be used until ancient fruit is finished being set up.

Professions

Farming - Tiller, Artisan
Higher wine sell price
Mining - Miner, Prospector
More coal
Foraging - Gatherer, Botanist
Higher truffle sell price
Fishing - Fisher, N/A
Fish don't sell for too much, so based on your needs
Combat - Scout, Acrobat
Hammer glitch

These are the most efficient professions to take, and if needed can be changed later on according to the player's needs through the Statue of Uncertainty found in the sewers

Wilderness

Fishing

Fishing isn't too profitable except in the early game, so fish only when you want to.

Skull Cavern

Iridium farming
Ignore all else, find the ore

Generally an uncommon method, but by going into skull caverns for iridium ore, you can amass a good amount of profit. You may want to choose the blacksmith profession for higher bar sell price, and use crystallariums for jades to trade for staircases.

Geodes and Artifact Troves

5 Omnigeodes = 1 artifact trove
Treasure chest, pearl, golden pumpkin

Not the most efficient way of obtaining profit, but you will have leftover geodes from skull caverns and the listed items above do sell for a decent amount of wealth.

Miscellaneous

Truffles

Pigs
Botanist = 1,750g

Yet again, truffles produce a huge amount of easy income, second only to crops/wine.

Honey

Spring
Blue Jazz = 280g
Summer
Poppy = 532g
Fall
Fairy Rose = 952g

Assuming you have artisan profession, you can get 40% more profit from honey for every yield each fourth day.

Crystallariums

Diamond
150g/day (+30%) = 195g/day

Not a common or incredibly profitable method, but usable nonetheless. It is recommended to have the gemologist profession when doing this for 30% extra profit from selling gems.

Fish Pond

Sturgeon
Caviar = 700g
Crimsonfish
Aged Roe = 2184g

A consistent form of profit with the artisan profession, though not as valuable as it takes up space that could be used for sheds and crops. The best fish for the ponds are sturgeon for caviar and crimsonfish for its consistency in producing roe and having the second highest fish price.
